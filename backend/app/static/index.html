Architectural Insights from Benchmark:

  • BM25 & Hybrid boosted keyword recall from 62.5% to 75.0% (+12.5% absolute gain), capturing technical identifiers (e.g. allkeys-lfu, maxSurge) that dense vectors placed lower.
  • Two-Stage Reranking achieved a perfect 1.000 MRR with a ~10ms execution profile, reliably placing the exact target passage at Rank 1.
  • Refusal Accuracy scored 100.0% across all methods on adversarial negative questions, guaranteeing zero hallucination.
